Stone rclone privacy policy

Stone rclone is a personal, single-user backup tool. Only its owner signs in to it.

  • It reads and writes only the Google Drive files it creates itself (the drive.file scope).
  • Files and sign-in tokens stay on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent to any third party.

To revoke access at any time, visit Google Account permissions.
